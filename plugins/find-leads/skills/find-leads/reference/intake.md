# Intake: the business and its ideal customer

## In a repo

1. Read `README.md` and any business overview it points to. Do not read code to guess the business.
2. Read `market-study.md` and its rival files if they exist: the gaps, the complaint themes, the rivals, and the
   hooks. Rivals' unhappy customers and the gaps are lead sources; the hooks feed the messages.
3. Read any existing list: a `prospects.md`, a `leads/` folder, a CSV of contacts. Note its leads (so none repeat),
   its rules (who qualifies, who is excluded) and its status values.
4. Write the **ideal customer profile** in 2 or 3 lines:
   - who they are (a kind of business, or a kind of person) and where;
   - the size or situation that makes them able to pay;
   - the sign that shows they need it now;
   - who is excluded.

   Example: "Independent bakeries and cafes in Austin with their own website and Instagram, busy enough to post
   daily specials, whose reviews mention long phone queues for orders. Excluded: chains, and cafes already
   using an online ordering app."
5. Show it in the first dialog: "Is this who to look for?" with "Yes", "Mostly, with a fix", "No, someone else".

## No repo, or no business in it

Ask in the first dialog (plain words, a recommended option where it makes sense):

1. **What you sell and to whom:** a sentence, or a link to the site or page.
2. **Where:** a city, a country, a region, or online and worldwide. This sets the sources and the languages.
3. **Businesses, people, or both:** who buys.
4. **Price and size:** what it costs, and who is too small to afford it.
5. **Known customers or rivals** (optional): names or links, so lookalikes can be found and rivals' unhappy
   customers searched.

Then the rest of the first dialog from SKILL.md, Step 2 (count, channels, exclusions, browser, paid tool, old list).

## Anything the person pasted

Links, screenshots and names given with the request are inputs: a competitor's ad points to its customers, a
screenshot of a post is an intent lead, a list of current clients is a lookalike source and an exclusion list.
