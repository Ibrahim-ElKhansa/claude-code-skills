# People rules: leads who are individuals

A lead who is a private person (a job seeker, a parent, a student, a shopper) gets stricter treatment than a
business. Privacy laws treat a person's name, phone, email and other identifying details as personal data:
for example the EU's GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and Lebanon's Law 81 of 2018 on electronic
transactions and personal data. Collecting them for selling without consent can be illegal, and it is the kind
of profiling people resent.

## What may be recorded

- The **public username or display name** shown on the post.
- The **link to the public post** and its date.
- **What they need**, in one line, in your own words (not a copy of their post).
- The platform and, if they said it in the post, the city or region.

## What is never recorded

- Phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, photos, workplaces, family details, age, or links to their other
  accounts, even when they are easy to find.
- Anything from a private group, a closed account, or a page that needs joining or following to read.
- Anything combined from several sources into a profile of the person.

## How a person may be reached

- **Only where they asked:** a reply on their public post or a DM on the same platform, the way a helpful person
  would answer. Never on another channel they did not offer.
- The message answers their request and says who is writing and why. No pressure, and it must be fine for them to
  ignore it.

## Businesses are different

A business lead may carry the business's public contact details (a business email, phone or WhatsApp number shown
on its own page or listing) and a decision maker's role title. A sole trader whose business details are their
personal details (a home phone, a personal email) is treated as a person.

## Keep lead files out of git

Lead files hold details about real people and businesses. They live in a `leads/` folder that is listed in the
repo's `.gitignore` (asked before adding) and are never committed or pushed. A person who asks to be removed is
deleted from `leads.csv`, not just marked.
