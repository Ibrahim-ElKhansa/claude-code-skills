# Messages: one tailored first message per lead

The person sends every message themselves. The skill only drafts.

## Shape

1. **The one real detail**, first: something true and specific about this lead, seen on their page or post
   ("Saw your new winter collection post", "You asked in the group on Monday about...").
2. **Who is writing and why**, in one line: the person's name or business and what they do.
3. **The offer**, in one line, tied to that detail or to their need. If a `market-study.md` exists, use its
   selling points and hooks.
4. **An easy next step:** a yes-or-no question, a short demo, a link to see it. One ask only.

Under about 80 words. No greeting paragraph, no long introduction, no list of features.

## Language

- In the language the lead uses on their page or post (Arabic, Arabizi, French, English...), with an English
  version beside it in `message_en` for the person to read.
- The tone of the channel: a WhatsApp or DM reads like a person; an email can be slightly more formal.

## Per channel

- **Email:** a short subject line that names the detail; ends with a one-line opt-out, for example "Not
  interested? Reply 'no' and I won't write again."
- **WhatsApp:** only to a number the business published for customers; no attachments in a first message.
- **Instagram or Facebook DM:** to the business page; for a person, a reply on their post or a DM on that same
  platform only.
- **LinkedIn:** a connection note under 300 characters, or a short InMail; no pitch in the first line.

## Honesty (always)

- Say who is writing and why. Never pretend to be a customer, a friend, a fan or a mutual contact.
- No fake urgency ("only today"), no fake discounts, no invented numbers, results or clients.
- Never claim something the product does not do. A message that relies on a feature not built yet is not drafted.
- No tricks: no "Re:" on a first email, no fake "following up" on a first message.
- People (not businesses): answer what they asked; no hard sell.

## Never

- No follow-ups are drafted.
- Nothing is sent by the skill, through any tool.
