# Helper brief: one rival

Give each helper agent this brief, filled in. Helpers run at most 5 at a time.

---

You are researching one competitor for a market study. Use web search and page reads only. Do not use a browser
tool, do not watch videos, do not sign in, sign up, post, buy or start a trial, and do not use any paid service.

**The niche we are studying:** {niche in one line}
**The launch market:** {country or region}, languages to search in: {languages}
**The rival:** {name}, {website or store link or "unknown"}
**What we already know:** {anything from the request, the repo or earlier studies, with dates}

Find, with a source URL for every fact:

1. **Who they are:** what they sell, to whom, in which countries and languages, who runs it, where they are based,
   when they started, size hints.
2. **Prices:** every plan, price, billing period, currency, free plan or trial, refund terms. "No public price" if
   none.
3. **Their pitch:** their headline promise and the 3 or 4 claims they repeat.
4. **Apps:** their App Store and Google Play pages in the launch country, if any: rating, number of ratings, download
   range, last update.
5. **Negative reviews and complaints:** from the app stores (Apple's public review feed included), Trustpilot,
   Google Maps results, G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, Reddit and forums, in the launch market's languages and English.
   Collect up to 30. For each: the gist in one line, a short quote (no reviewer name), the source, the date, and the
   star rating if any.
6. **Strengths:** what their happy customers praise (up to 5, sourced), so we know what not to compete on.
7. **Ad and social traces** visible without a browser: ads quoted in articles, their public follower counts,
   YouTube videos.

Tag every fact: **theirs** (their own claim), **seen** (seen on a page, store or review), **ours** (your estimate).
Never invent a number, a quote or a review. Unknown stays "unknown".

Return, in this order:

- a 3-line summary: who they are, how much they matter to this niche (high, medium, low) and why;
- the facts above, as short bullets, each with its tag and URL;
- the complaints as a list: gist, quote (original language plus an English translation if needed), source, date,
  stars;
- what you could not check and why (blocked page, login, no data).

Keep it under about 800 words.
