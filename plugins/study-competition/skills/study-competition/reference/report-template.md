# Report templates

Plain words, short sentences, tables where they help. Every fact carries its tag (**theirs**, **seen**, **ours**)
and a source. Dates are written out (22 October 2026), never relative ("last week").

## market-study.md

```markdown
# Market study: {niche in a few words}

_Study of {date}, depth {Quick or Full}. Niche: {the confirmed one-line niche}. Launch market: {place}. Searched in:
{languages}. Browser: {used, look only | not used}. Videos watched: {n, or "none, watch not installed"}._

## Since {last study's date}
(Only on a re-run: new rivals, price changes, new or stopped ads, new complaint themes, trends that turned.)

## In short
{5 to 8 lines: the market in one sentence, who matters most, the biggest complaint, the clearest gap, the one move
we would make first.}

## The rivals
| Rival | What they sell | Price | Where | Strength | Weakness | Matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name](rival-name.md) | ... | $X a month (seen) | ... | ... | ... | high, medium or low |

Indirect rivals and doing-it-by-hand: {short list}.

## What customers complain about
| Theme | Count | Rivals it hits | Example (short quote, source) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | 14 (ours) | A, B | "..." (Trustpilot, March 2026) |

## Gaps nobody fills
- {gap}: {evidence}

## Selling points and hooks
| Complaint | What we do instead | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | "..." |

{Any hook that names a rival carries: "Names a rival: check your country's ad rules and the platform's policy
first."}

## How rivals advertise
{Their messages, offers and prices in ads, the longest-running ads and what that suggests, and the videos watched:
hook, offer, call to action. Without a browser: the filled ad library links.}

## Trends
{Each keyword: direction over 5 years and 12 months, seasonality, rising related searches. Or the filled links and
"not read: no browser".}

## Abroad
(Full depth or abroad mode: the table from `abroad.md` and 3 angles to try at home.)

## What to do
- **Improve the current idea:** 3 to 5 changes, each tied to a complaint or a gap.
- **New or pivoted ideas:** 2 or 3, each with why it has a chance and its main risk.
(For pure market research: the opportunities, ranked.)

## Could not check
- {what, why}

## Sources
- {URL} (read {date})
```

## Writing hooks

- A hook is the first line of a short video ad: under about 12 words, spoken or on screen in the first 2 seconds.
- Each hook answers one real complaint from the table, in the customer's own words where possible ("87 applications,
  0 replies?").
- Shapes that work: a question about the pain, a blunt claim of the fix, a number, a before and after, "stop
  {pain}".
- Write 2 or 3 hooks per top complaint, in the launch market's language and in English.
- Never promise what the product does not do. A hook for a feature not built yet is marked "needs {feature}".
- No invented numbers in hooks ("20 more sites", "1,000 posts"). A number in a hook is one the study found or the
  business states; otherwise write it without a number.
