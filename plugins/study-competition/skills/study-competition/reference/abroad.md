# Abroad: where else is this already a niche

Runs in Full studies and in abroad mode. It finds where the same idea already works in another market, and whether
the home market has it yet. A niche that is big abroad and thin at home is an opening.

## Where the method comes from

An Instagram reel called "Chinese Hormozi Theory" ("Hormozi" is Alex Hormozi, the business-content creator). Its
advice, near word for word: whatever niche you are in, use AI to find the Chinese version of who you aspire to be,
take their 10 best-performing videos and make the English version. The reel claimed it would take 50 accounts to a
million followers; that claim is the creator's own and unverified, so treat the method as a research prompt, not a
proven result.

Two changes on purpose:

1. **It is global.** China is one market in the list, not the only one, and the idea can be any business, not only a
   creator to copy.
2. **It never copies.** "Straight ripping" someone's videos is copyright infringement and invites takedowns. Take
   the idea, the structure and the format; the remake is original.

## Steps

1. **Restate the idea in one line**, using the niche from Step 1 (or the abroad request).
2. **Pick the markets.** Default: China, South Korea, Japan, India, Brazil, Russia, Turkey, the Arab world, Southeast
   Asia, Europe, and the United States when the home market is elsewhere. Drop the home market itself. Add or drop
   markets when the person names some.
3. **Search each market in its own language and on its own platforms**, not only in English: Douyin, Bilibili,
   Xiaohongshu and Kuaishou (China); Naver and KakaoTalk channels (Korea); Niconico and note (Japan); VK (Russia);
   Kwai (Brazil); local app stores and marketplaces everywhere. Public search results and public pages only; a page
   behind a login is read only through the browser, with permission.
4. **For each market find:** who leads the niche there, which formats, features and offers work, what they charge,
   a rough audience or customer size, and whether the same thing exists in the home market or in English.
5. **Report** (the "Abroad" section of `market-study.md`): a table of market, who or what, why it works there, the
   gap at home, confidence (high, medium, low). Then 3 concrete angles to try at home, each saying what it borrows
   (an idea, a format, a feature, a pricing model) and what is original.
6. **Originality check:** when an angle sits close to one specific creator's or company's work, say so.

## What it does not do

It does not download, translate or re-upload anyone's videos, and it contacts no one. Watching a foreign video ad
(Step 6 of the skill) is fine for understanding it; the remake is always new.
