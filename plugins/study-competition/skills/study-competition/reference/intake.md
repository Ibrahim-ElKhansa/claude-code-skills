# Intake: learning the niche

## In a repo

1. Read `README.md`. If it points to a business overview (a "what it is" section, `docs/`, a pitch or a notes file),
   read that too. Do not read code to guess the business.
2. Write the niche in one line: **what** is sold, **to whom**, **where**, **price**, **stage**. Example: "A meal
   prep delivery service for office workers in Austin, $12 a meal, live since March 2026."
3. Note any rivals the README already names, and any existing rivals folder or earlier `market-study.md`.
4. In the first dialog, show the line and ask: "Is this right?" with options "Yes", "Mostly, with a fix (say what)",
   and "No, it's something else". Ask only the intake questions below that the README does not answer.

## No repo, or no niche in it

Ask these in the first dialog (one question each, plain words, a recommended option where one makes sense):

1. **The idea and who it is for.** "What do you sell (or plan to sell), and to whom?" Offer to accept a sentence,
   a link to their site or a pitch.
2. **Where it launches.** A country or city, a region, or online and global. This decides which rivals count and
   which languages to search in.
3. **Stage and goal.** Stage: just an idea, built but not launched, live, or no business (pure market research).
   Goal: improve the current idea, find a new idea in this niche, or only understand the market.
4. **Price and known rivals.** Their planned or current price (or "not decided"), and any competitors they already
   know (names or links). Both are optional.

## Always in the same first dialog

- **Depth** (study mode only): Quick (about 5 rivals, about 15 minutes) or Full (10 or more rivals, every source, the
  abroad stage, 45 to 60 minutes). Recommend Quick for a first look and Full for a decision with money behind it.
- **Browser permission:** "May I look at pages in your browser to read ad libraries, Google Trends and comments? Look
  only: I never click save, send, post or buy." Ask only if a browser tool is available.

## Anything the person pasted

Links, screenshots and videos given with the request are inputs, not noise: an ad is evidence about a rival, a
screenshot of a review is a complaint to count. Read screenshots directly. Video links go to Step 6.
